How it works
➕ Adding items
  • •Type any item and tap Add or press Enter
  • •As you type, it suggests items from your past receipts — so after a few scans it knows your usual products
  • •Tap a suggestion to instantly add it with the correct category already filled in
🛒 While shopping
  • •Items are grouped by category so you can shop the whole aisle at once
  • •Tap the circle next to any item to tick it off — it moves to a "Got it" section at the bottom
  • •A progress bar shows how far through your list you are (e.g. "6 of 10 items got — 60%")
🧹 Cleaning up
  • •Clear ticked items removes everything you've already got
  • •Clear all wipes the list for a fresh start

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026

Your Privacy Matters

SlipSmart is designed with privacy and data ownership as core principles. We only collect information necessary to provide the services offered by the application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Country selection
  • Currency preference

Receipt Data

When you scan or upload receipts, we may store:

  • Store names
  • Purchase dates
  • Purchase amounts
  • Receipt items
  • Categories and tags
  • Tax-related information entered by you

Receipt Images

Receipt images uploaded through SlipSmart are securely stored and processed to provide receipt management, search, reporting, warranty tracking, and tax-related features.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to provide and improve SlipSmart features, including:

  • Receipt storage
  • Receipt search
  • Budget tracking
  • Tax reporting
  • Warranty management
  • PDF exports
  • Data backup and recovery

We do not sell your personal information.
We do not share your data with advertisers.
We do not use your receipt data for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

SlipSmart uses trusted third-party service providers to operate the platform. These may include:

  • Supabase (authentication and database services)
  • Supabase (image storage and delivery)
  • AI/OCR providers for receipt data extraction

These providers process data only as necessary to deliver the requested functionality.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. Data is protected through:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Cloud provider security measures

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

Your receipts and account information remain stored until:

  • You delete individual receipts
  • You delete your account
  • Retention is required by law

Your Rights

You remain the owner of your data. You may:

  • Access your information
  • Export your receipts and reports
  • Download your receipt archive
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data

Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time using the Delete Account feature within the application. Upon deletion, we will take reasonable steps to remove your personal information and associated receipt data from our systems.

Children's Privacy

SlipSmart is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the application and become effective upon publication.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact: support.slipsmart@gmail.com

Tag Your Receipts
Choose the tags that apply to each receipt. You can select more than one tag. These tags help SlipSmart organise your receipts and generate accurate tax, warranty and expense reports.
What do these tags mean?
🏠
Personal
Everyday purchases for personal use. These are normally not tax claimable.
💼
Business
Purchases made for business activities or self-employment.
🏥
Medical
Medical expenses such as pharmacy purchases, doctor visits, prescriptions and healthcare-related costs.
📋
Tax Claimable
Expenses that may be claimable on a tax return. These receipts will be included in tax reports and tax packs.
🛡️
Warranty
Items that may require proof of purchase in the future, such as electronics, appliances, tools or equipment.
💡 You can select multiple tags
For example, a pharmacy receipt could be both Medical and Tax Claimable. A laptop purchase could be Business, Tax Claimable and Warranty.
